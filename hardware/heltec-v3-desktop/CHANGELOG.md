# Changelog

## V1 — Initial Prototype

- Three-part design: main body, floating PCB tray, lid
- M3 heat inserts in corner pillars
- Micro slide switch in rounded boss on back face, inserts from interior
- SMA bulkhead in boss on right wall, hex nut pocket on interior
- OLED window + 2x button holes in lid
- USB-C slot on left wall
- u.FL pigtail notch in tray
- Battery foam tape pad recesses in floor
- Rounded exterior corners

### Known issues
- Heat inserts pulled out on first assembly — pillar height adjusted post-print to fix USB-C slot alignment
- No screw depth stop — easy to over-drive screws

## V2 — Planned
- Screw length stop in pillar bores
- Lid underside PCB ribs
- Heltec V4 tray + lid variant
- Mirrored body variant (antenna and USB-C on opposite sides) — allows screen to face up in installations where current orientation puts screen upside down
